About Aroma & Brew

Founded in 2026, Aroma & Brew Coffee Co. brings ethically sourced, locally harvested beans directly to your cup. Whether you need a morning boost or a quiet cozy corner to study and work, our shop offers high-quality espresso, crafted beverages, and delicious warm pastries.

Featured Drinks

Signature Espresso

Iced Vanilla Oat Latte
